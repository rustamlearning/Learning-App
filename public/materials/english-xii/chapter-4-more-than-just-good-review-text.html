2. Situational Hook

Viral Is Not Always Meaningful

Di kelas XII, guru menampilkan poster, trailer description, dan sinopsis singkat dari karya media Indonesia yang sedang banyak dibicarakan. Setelah melihatnya, beberapa siswa langsung berkata:

“This film is good.”
“I like it.”
“It is viral.”
“The animation is cool.”
“The song is nice.”

Guru kemudian bertanya: “Good in what way?” dan “Is popular always the same as good?” Siswa mulai sadar bahwa review tidak cukup hanya mengatakan good, bad, viral, atau interesting.

Think First

What films, series, songs, or videos are popular in Indonesia now?
Is viral content always meaningful?
How can we criticize a work politely?
What is the difference between “I like it” and “I like it because…”?
What kind of media would you recommend to students?
How can students in remote islands access media offline-first?
3. Learning Objectives

Tujuan Pembelajaran

  1. Memahami fungsi sosial Review Text untuk menilai, mengapresiasi, dan merekomendasikan karya/media.
  2. Mengidentifikasi struktur orientation → interpretative recount/summary → evaluation → evaluative summation/recommendation.
  3. Menjelaskan title, type, genre, theme, character, plot/content, message, strengths, weaknesses, audience, dan cultural relevance.
  4. Menggunakan opinion expressions: In my opinion, I think, I believe, For me, From my point of view.

  6. Memberikan kritik secara sopan dan seimbang.
  7. Menulis review text sederhana dan menyampaikan oral review pendek.
  8. Membuat digital/offline review card atau media poster.
  9. Merefleksikan hubungan antara media populer, nilai karakter, budaya Indonesia, dan kehidupan remaja.

5. MoT — Modelling of the Text

A Review of Jumbo: More Than Just a Popular Animated Film

Teacher source note: Model ini memakai film animasi Indonesia Jumbo sebagai contoh karya aktual. Fakta umum yang digunakan: Jumbo adalah film animasi Indonesia tahun 2025 yang disutradarai Ryan Adriandhy; Reuters melaporkan film ini menyalip Frozen 2 sebagai film animasi terlaris di Indonesia, dan Kompas melaporkan film ini menembus lebih dari 10 juta penonton. Gunakan fakta secukupnya; fokus utama materi tetap pada struktur review dan literasi media.

Orientation

Jumbo is an Indonesian animated film directed by Ryan Adriandhy. It became one of the most talked-about Indonesian films in 2025 because it showed that a local animated film could attract wide public attention. The film is often discussed not only because of its box-office success, but also because it represents the growth of Indonesian animation and local storytelling. In my opinion, Jumbo is meaningful for students to review because it gives us a chance to talk about courage, friendship, bullying, family, and the power of Indonesian creative work.

Interpretative Recount / Summary

The film tells the story of Don, a boy who faces personal struggles and social pressure. He is often judged by others, but he still tries to show his talent and prove that he has value. The story includes emotional moments, fantasy elements, and scenes about friendship and courage. Without telling every detail, the film follows Don’s journey as he deals with hurtful treatment, tries to understand himself, and learns that he does not have to be defined by other people’s negative words.

The story is easy to connect with teenage life. Many students know what it feels like to be judged, laughed at, ignored, or misunderstood. Because of that, the film can open a useful discussion about bullying, self-confidence, and emotional safety. Students in different places, including remote islands like Sapuka, can understand the main issue because the feeling of wanting to be accepted is universal.

Evaluation

The strongest part of Jumbo is its message. The film reminds viewers that people should not be judged only by appearance, nickname, weakness, or other people’s labels. This message is important for teenagers because school life can sometimes include jokes, teasing, or social pressure. The film can help students think about how words affect someone’s confidence.

Another strength is the emotional impact. The story is not only entertaining; it also invites viewers to care about the main character. The emotional scenes help the audience understand Don’s feelings. For students, this is important because media should not only make people laugh or follow a trend. Good media can also build empathy.

The visual style is also one of the film’s strengths. As an Indonesian animated film, Jumbo gives students an example that Indonesian creators can produce animation that many people appreciate. This can inspire young people who like drawing, storytelling, editing videos, or creating digital content. For students in Sapuka, this is a useful reminder that creativity is not limited to big cities. A good idea can come from anywhere.

The film is also relevant to media literacy. Because it became very popular, students may think, “It is viral, so it must be good.” However, a smart viewer should ask deeper questions: What is the message? What values does it show? Is it suitable for teenagers? What are its strengths and weaknesses? By asking these questions, students learn not only to consume media, but also to evaluate it.

However, Jumbo is not perfect. Some viewers may feel that certain emotional scenes are predictable. Some parts may need teacher guidance, especially if students want to discuss bullying and emotional pressure in a deeper way. The fantasy elements may also feel unusual for students who prefer realistic stories. Still, these weaknesses do not remove the value of the film. They can become discussion points in class.

Evaluative Summation / Recommendation

Overall, I recommend Jumbo to students, teachers, and families, especially if they want to discuss animation, bullying, self-confidence, and Indonesian creativity. The film is entertaining, emotional, and meaningful. However, students should watch it critically, not only because it is popular. They should ask what the film teaches, what parts are strong, and what parts can be improved. For me, Jumbo is worth discussing because it proves that a popular film can also become a meaningful learning material.

7. Language Features & Grammar Lab

Grammar Lab: Review with Reasons

A. Opinion Expressions

Pattern: I think + the work + is + adjective + because + reason.

  • I think this film is inspiring because it shows courage.
  • In my opinion, this video is useful because it teaches online safety.
  • I recommend this song to teenagers because the message is positive.
B. Evaluative Adjectives

Positive: inspiring, meaningful, touching, powerful, realistic, entertaining, memorable, creative, relevant.

Balanced/Critical: simple, predictable, slow, unclear, confusing, too quick, less natural, overrated, repetitive.

C. Polite Strengths & Weaknesses
  • One strength of this work is...
  • The strongest part is...
  • One weakness is...
  • The work would be better if...
  • Although some parts are predictable, the message is still meaningful.
D. Simple Present vs Simple Past

Simple present for evaluation: The film shows courage. The song gives a positive message.

Simple past for summary: The character faced a problem. The video explained the issue clearly.

Common Mistakes

This film very good.This film is very good.Perlu to be “is”.

9. More Model Texts

More Model Texts

A Review of an Inspiring Short Video

Orientation The short video “Study First, Scroll Later” is an educational video about students and phone habits. In my opinion, this video is useful for teenagers because many students now spend a lot of time scrolling on social media.

Summary The video shows a student who wants to study, but his phone keeps distracting him. After opening many short videos, he realizes that his homework is not finished.

Evaluation The strongest part is the simple message: phones can help learning, but they can also distract us. One weakness is that the video is too short and needs more practical tips.

Recommendation I recommend this video to students because it is worth discussing in class.

  1. What is the video about?
  2. What is one weakness?
  3. Can a short video change students’ habits?

A Review of a Popular Indonesian Song

Orientation This review is about a popular Indonesian song with a positive message about hope and personal strength. I will not quote the lyrics, but I will discuss the message.

Summary The song tells listeners that life can be difficult, but people should not give up. The music creates a warm and emotional atmosphere.

Evaluation The strongest part is its emotional impact. The song is meaningful because many teenagers sometimes feel pressure from school, family, friendship, or the future. One weakness is that the message is quite general.

Recommendation I recommend this song to teenagers who like emotional songs with positive messages.

  1. What is the song’s general message?
  2. Why is it suitable for teenagers?
  3. Why should students discuss song messages?

A Review of an Indonesian Animated Film

Orientation Jumbo is an Indonesian animated film that became widely discussed in 2025. It is worth reviewing because it is entertaining, emotional, and connected to issues teenagers understand.

Summary The film focuses on a boy who faces hurtful treatment and social pressure. He wants to show that he has value even when others judge him unfairly.

Evaluation One strength is the message: do not judge people by appearance or labels. Another strength is the animation, which can inspire students who like digital storytelling. However, some scenes may feel predictable and need teacher guidance.

Recommendation I recommend this film to students and families because it teaches empathy, courage, and appreciation for Indonesian creative work.

  1. Why is Jumbo worth reviewing?
  2. What message does it teach?
  3. Why should students not follow trends blindly?

A Review of a Recent Indonesian Series

Orientation This review discusses a recent Indonesian series that focuses on youth, family, friendship, and personal choices. It can be used for classroom discussion if the teacher chooses appropriate scenes or summaries.

Summary The series follows young characters who face problems in school, family, and friendship. They must make choices about honesty, dreams, relationships, and responsibility.

Evaluation The strongest part is its connection to youth life. Many teenagers understand pressure, fear of failure, and the need to make family proud. However, some scenes may include mature topics, so teacher guidance is important.

Recommendation I recommend selected parts of this kind of series, not necessarily the full series, for guided classroom discussion.

  1. What issues does the series discuss?
  2. Why should teachers choose appropriate scenes?
  3. How can students watch series critically?

My Review of a Campaign Video

Orientation I watched a campaign video about online safety. The video was short, but the message was important.

Summary The video showed a student receiving a suspicious link from an unknown account. A friend reminded him to check the source before clicking.

Evaluation The strongest part is the practical message: do not share personal data and do not click suspicious links. The visual style is simple and clear. One weakness is that it does not explain what to do after clicking a suspicious link by mistake.

Recommendation I recommend this video to students because it teaches online safety and connects with netiquette and digital finance.

  1. What is the video about?
  2. What should be improved?
  3. What should students do before clicking a link?
10. Listening

Oral Review Script: Why I Recommend This Indonesian Film

Speaker: A student reviewer

Good morning, everyone. Today, I will review an Indonesian animated film titled Jumbo. This film is about a boy who faces social pressure and tries to prove that he has value. The story is emotional and easy to understand. It talks about courage, friendship, self-confidence, and the danger of judging people unfairly.

In my opinion, the strongest part of the film is its message. The film reminds us that words can hurt people. It also teaches us to respect others and not judge someone only by appearance or nickname. This message is important for students because school life should be emotionally safe.

However, the film is not perfect. Some scenes may feel predictable, and some topics need teacher guidance, especially when discussing bullying and emotional pressure. Overall, I recommend this film to students and families. It is entertaining, but it is also worth discussing. Students should not watch it only because it is popular. They should watch it critically.

Shared Writing Example

A Review of an Online Safety Campaign Video

An online safety campaign video is a short educational video about how students should protect themselves on the internet. The video is suitable for teenagers because many students use phones, social media, online games, and digital payment every day.

The video tells the story of a student who receives a suspicious link from an unknown account. The message says that he can get a prize if he clicks the link and fills in his personal data. At first, the student feels excited. However, his friend reminds him to check the source first.

The strongest part of the video is its clear message. It teaches students not to share personal data, passwords, or OTP codes. One weakness is that the video is quite short. It would be better if it explained how to report suspicious accounts.

Overall, I recommend this video to students because it teaches online safety in a simple way. It is worth watching and discussing in class. I give it 4 out of 5 stars.
