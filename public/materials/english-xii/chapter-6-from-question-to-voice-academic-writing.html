Situational Hook — From Opinion to Research

Di akhir kelas XII, guru memberi pertanyaan:

“What problem in student life is important to study?”

Siswa memberi jawaban berbeda: “Many students use AI now,” “Some students scroll social media too much,” “Some students delay assignments,” “Internet helps us learn, but sometimes it distracts us,” dan “Plastic waste around school is still a problem.”

Guru lalu berkata: “Good. But academic writing is not only about opinion. You need a question, data, evidence, and a clear argument.”

MoT — Modelling of the Text

How Does AI Affect Students’ English Learning Habits?

Class Survey ExampleThesisDataAcademic Integrity

Note: This essay is based on a small class survey of 20 students. The data is only an example for learning academic writing. It should not be used to describe all students in Indonesia.

Introduction

Artificial intelligence, or AI, is becoming part of students’ learning habits. Many students now use AI through mobile phones, chat applications, learning websites, or social media tools. In English learning, AI can help students find vocabulary, translate sentences, check grammar, summarize texts, and create practice questions. For students in remote areas, AI may become useful because learning resources are not always easy to access. However, AI also creates new problems. Some students may copy AI answers without understanding them. Others may become too dependent on AI when doing assignments.

This topic is important for Grade XII students because they need to prepare for college, work, and future learning. Students should know how to use technology wisely and honestly. To understand this issue, a small class survey was conducted with 20 students. The survey asked about AI use in English learning, the purpose of using AI, the benefits, the risks, and the need for teacher guidance.

Thesis Statement

This essay argues that AI can support students’ English learning, especially in vocabulary, translation, and grammar revision, but students still need teacher guidance to avoid copying, dependence, and dishonest learning habits.

Body Paragraph 1 — AI as Learning Support

The survey shows that many students have started using AI for English learning. Based on the class survey, 15 of 20 students said they had used AI to help them learn English. This suggests that AI is no longer a strange tool for many students. It has become part of how some students search for help when they do not understand English materials.

The most common use of AI was vocabulary learning. According to the survey result, 12 of 20 students used AI to find the meaning of difficult English words. This can be helpful because vocabulary is one of the biggest problems for beginner English learners. When students do not understand many words, they may stop reading or feel afraid to write. AI can give meanings, example sentences, and simple explanations. For example, a student can ask AI to explain the word “evidence” and use it in a sentence.

The survey also found that 10 students used AI to translate sentences, and 9 students used AI to check grammar. These findings show that students often use AI as a language assistant. Translation can help students understand difficult sentences, while grammar checking can help them revise writing. However, students should not depend on translation for every sentence. They still need to learn how the sentence works. AI is useful when students use it to understand, not when they use it to avoid thinking.

Body Paragraph 2 — Risks of Copying and Dependence

Although AI can support learning, the survey also shows some risks. Based on the class survey, 6 of 20 students admitted that they had copied AI answers at least once. This finding is important because copying does not build real understanding. A paragraph may look correct, but the student may not be able to explain the meaning. This can become a problem in class when the teacher asks students to present or answer questions about their own writing.

Copying AI answers can also reduce academic honesty. Academic writing should show the student’s own thinking, even if the grammar is simple. If students submit AI text as their own work, they may get a good-looking answer but lose the chance to learn. Honest writing is better than perfect-looking copying.

Another risk is dependence. Some students may ask AI for every sentence, every idea, and every correction. This may make them less confident in writing independently. AI may help students revise, but it should not replace their voice. Students need to practice making sentences, organizing paragraphs, and explaining ideas in their own words.

Body Paragraph 3 — The Need for Teacher Guidance

The survey shows that students need clear rules about AI use. According to the survey result, 17 of 20 students agreed that teachers should give clear rules for using AI in learning. This finding suggests that students may be more responsible when they understand what is allowed and what is not allowed.

Teacher guidance can help students use AI as a learning assistant. For example, teachers can allow students to use AI for brainstorming ideas, checking vocabulary, or revising grammar. However, teachers can also explain that students must not copy full essays, create fake sources, or submit AI answers without understanding them. A simple rule can help: try first, ask second, check again, and write yourself.

For students in remote areas, teacher guidance is even more important. Internet access may help students find more learning materials, but not all students have the same device, signal, or quota. Therefore, schools should design AI tasks fairly. Students should be able to complete assignments even with limited internet access.

Conclusion

In conclusion, AI affects students’ English learning habits in both helpful and risky ways. The class survey shows that many students use AI for vocabulary, translation, and grammar revision. These uses can support English learning, especially for students who need extra explanation. However, the survey also shows that some students have copied AI answers, which can reduce understanding and academic honesty.

The findings show that AI should not be banned completely, but it should not be used carelessly. Students should use AI to learn, revise, and practice, not to replace their thinking. Teachers should give clear rules, and students should write in their own words. AI can become a useful learning assistant when students use it honestly, critically, and responsibly.

F. Avoiding Plagiarism

Do Not

  • Copy full text.
  • Change only one word.
  • Submit AI text as your own.
  • Use fake sources or fake data.

Do

  • Understand first.
  • Write in your own words.
  • Mention the source.
  • Use data honestly.
  • Ask the teacher if unsure.

More Model Essays

Model Essay A — Very Easy: Do Students Use AI to Learn Vocabulary?

Introduction: Many students find English vocabulary difficult. Some students now use AI to find meanings and examples of new words. This essay discusses how students use AI to learn vocabulary. The essay is based on a small survey of 10 students.

Thesis: AI can help students learn vocabulary, but students should still write examples in their own words.

Body: Based on the survey, 8 of 10 students said they had used AI to find the meaning of English words. This suggests that AI is useful when students meet difficult vocabulary. However, 4 students said they sometimes copied AI examples without changing them. This can be a problem because students may not really understand the words.

Conclusion: AI can support vocabulary learning, but students should understand the meaning and create their own sentences.

  1. What is the essay about?
  2. How many students used AI for vocabulary?
  3. Why should students write their own examples?
Model Essay B — Easy: Does Social Media Reduce Students’ Study Focus?

Introduction: Social media is part of students’ daily life. Students use it to communicate, watch videos, and find information. However, social media may also distract students from studying. This essay is based on a class survey of 15 students.

Thesis: Social media can support learning when students use it wisely, but it may reduce study focus when students spend too much time scrolling.

Body: 13 of 15 students said they used social media every day. Some use it to watch learning videos or receive school information. However, 9 of 15 students said social media sometimes distracted them from homework. Notifications, short videos, and online chats may make students delay assignments. 11 students agreed that screen-time rules are needed.

Conclusion: Social media has both benefits and risks. Students should use it wisely and make clear study rules.

  1. How many students use social media every day?
  2. What is one benefit?
  3. What is one risk?
Model Essay C — Medium: Why Do Students Delay Assignments?

Introduction: Assignment delay is a common issue in many schools. It should not be explained with one simple label such as “lazy.” This essay discusses why students delay assignments based on a short interview with five students.

Thesis: Assignment delay may be influenced by phone use, low motivation, time management problems, and family responsibilities.

Body: Three of five students said they often planned to do homework but spent too much time on social media or online games. Two students said they delayed assignments because they did not understand the task. Two students also said they sometimes helped their parents after school, so they had less time to finish homework.

Conclusion: Students delay assignments for different reasons. Schools can help by giving clearer instructions, checking progress, and teaching better time management.

  1. Why should we avoid labeling students as lazy?
  2. What factors are mentioned?
  3. How can teachers help students?
Model Essay D — Local Context: How Does Internet Access Affect Learning in Remote Islands?

Introduction: Internet access can influence how students learn. In remote islands, students may use the internet to watch learning videos, search for information, use AI tools, or communicate with teachers. However, internet access is not always equal.

Thesis: Internet access can support students’ learning in remote islands, but unequal access, limited devices, and electricity problems may create learning challenges.

Body: Internet access can give students more learning resources. Students can search for English vocabulary, watch explanation videos, and practice with online tools. However, some students may have better phones, stronger signal, or more data packages than others. Another challenge is distraction. Internet access does not always mean better learning if students mostly use it for online games or doom-scrolling.

Conclusion: Internet access can improve learning in remote islands, but it must be used responsibly. Schools should provide offline alternatives when possible.

  1. How can internet access support learning?
  2. Why can unequal access become a problem?
  3. Why does internet access need discipline?
Model Essay E — Future Readiness: Why Do Students Need Digital Skills After Graduation?

Introduction: After graduation, students may continue to college, work, join training, start small businesses, or help their community. Digital skills are becoming more important.

Thesis: Students need digital skills after graduation because these skills can support learning, work, communication, creativity, and problem solving.

Body: Digital skills can support further study because students may need to search for sources, write assignments, and make presentations. They can also support work readiness because many jobs now require basic technology use. Digital skills can help students contribute to their community by making educational posters, promoting local products, or sharing school campaigns.

Conclusion: Digital skills are important, but students must use technology responsibly. Future readiness is not only about using tools, but also about using them wisely.

  1. What are digital skills?
  2. How can they support work?
  3. Why must they be combined with responsibility?

Listening Section — Mini Research Presentation

Script: How AI Affects Our English Learning Habits

Good morning, everyone. Today, I will present our mini research titled “How AI Affects Our English Learning Habits.” Our research question is: How does AI affect students’ English learning habits?

We collected data by using a class survey. Twenty students answered five questions about AI use in English learning.

The first finding is that many students have used AI for English learning. Based on the survey, 15 of 20 students said they had used AI. The second finding is about the purpose of using AI. Twelve students used AI for vocabulary, ten students used it for translation, and nine students used it to check grammar. The third finding is about risk. Six students admitted that they had copied AI answers at least once. Seventeen students agreed that teachers should give clear rules for AI use.

In conclusion, AI can help students learn English, especially vocabulary, translation, and grammar. However, students should not copy AI answers. We recommend that students try first, ask AI second, check again, and write in their own words. Thank you.

Dialogue A — Turning Opinion into Research Question

Alya: I want to write about social media. My opinion is, “Social media is bad.”

Rafi: That sounds too general. Can we make it a research question?

Alya: Maybe: “Is social media bad for students?”

Alya: Good idea. “How does social media affect students’ study focus?”

Dialogue B — How to Cite a Source

Student: Ma’am, I read an article about AI for learning. How can I use it?

Teacher: You can write, “According to the article provided by the teacher...” Do not copy the full sentence. Understand the idea and write it in your own words.

Student: So I should paraphrase and mention the source?

Teacher: Yes. Paraphrase and mention the source.

Student: So our thesis should show benefit and risk.

Shared Writing Example — Does Social Media Reduce Students’ Study Focus?

Social media is part of students’ daily life. Many students use social media to communicate, watch videos, and find information. However, social media may also reduce study focus when students use it too much. This essay discusses whether social media reduces students’ study focus based on a class survey of 15 students.

This essay argues that social media can support learning, but it may reduce study focus if students do not control their screen time.

Based on the survey, 13 of 15 students said they use social media every day. This shows that social media is a normal part of students’ lives. Students use it for communication, entertainment, and information. Because of this, teachers and students should not ignore its influence.

The survey also shows that 9 of 15 students said social media sometimes distracts them from homework. This suggests that social media can reduce study focus. Notifications, short videos, and online chats may make students delay assignments. This can be a problem when students open social media before finishing their schoolwork.

However, social media is not always negative. Based on the data, 6 of 15 students said they use social media to find learning videos. This means social media can also support learning when students use it for educational content.

In conclusion, social media can both help and distract students. The findings show that students need screen-time rules. Students should finish important school tasks first, turn off unnecessary notifications, and use social media for useful learning purposes.
